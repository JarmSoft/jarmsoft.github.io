Privacy Policy

Loan & Mortgage Calculator (com.jarmsoft.loancalculator)

Last Updated: September 2026

Summary of Our Privacy Commitment

Loan & Mortgage Calculator is built with a strict privacy-first, offline architecture. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share your personal or financial information. All mortgage estimations, loan parameters, amortization schedules, and settings remain solely on your device.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how JarmSoft ("we", "our", or "us") manages information within the Loan & Mortgage Calculator application for Android and iOS. This policy complies with Google Play Store Developer Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable international privacy regulations.

2. Financial Services & Personal Loans Policy Statement

⚠️ Regulatory & Financial Notice: Loan & Mortgage Calculator is an independent calculation and simulation utility tool designed for educational, informational, and personal planning purposes only. The Application is NOT a bank, lender, credit broker, or loan provider.

In full compliance with Google Play's Financial Services and Personal Loans Policy:

  • No Loan Disbursement: The application does not issue loans, originate credit lines, or disburse funds.
  • No Brokerage or Lead Generation: The application does not partner with or forward user information to third-party lenders, brokers, or financial institutions.
  • No Financial Advice: Calculations and graphs provided in the app are mathematical simulations based on standard amortization formulas. They do not constitute certified financial or mortgage advice. Actual loan costs depend on specific lender terms, taxes, insurance, and fees.

3. Zero Data Collection & Offline Storage

We believe your personal financial deliberations are private. As such:

  • No Cloud Accounts or Logins: You do not need an account, email address, phone number, or login credentials to use the app.
  • Local Calculations: All calculations (monthly payments, interest accrual, extra principal contributions) are performed locally on your device's processor using client-side algorithms.
  • Local Preferences: App preferences (such as selected currency symbol, theme mode, and default loan values) are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences and never transmitted over the internet.

4. PDF Report Generation & Sharing

The Application includes a feature allowing users to export or print comprehensive amortization tables as PDF documents.

  • On-Device Generation: PDF reports are rendered locally on your device using the pdf and printing libraries.
  • No Server Upload: PDF reports are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • User-Controlled Sharing: If you choose to share or print a PDF report via Android/iOS system dialogs (e.g., email, messaging apps, or cloud storage), that transfer occurs strictly through your device's native sharing sheet under your direct control.

5. Permissions Requested

In accordance with data minimization principles, Loan & Mortgage Calculator does not request sensitive permissions:

  • No Contact Access: We do not access your contacts.
  • No Location Access: We do not request or track your GPS or network location.
  • No Camera or Microphone: We do not access your cameras or recording equipment.
  • No Sensitive Storage Access: The app does not access your photos, media, or private external files.

6. Advertising, Analytics, Crash Diagnostics & In-App Purchases

To support the free ("Lite") version of the Application and continuously improve reliability, the Application integrates the following trusted third-party SDKs:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Displays banner and rewarded advertisements in the free version. AdMob may use the device's Advertising ID, coarse device signals, and ad interaction metrics to serve relevant or non-personalized ads in compliance with Google EU User Consent Policy (GDPR) and CCPA. Users who upgrade to Pro have all advertisements permanently disabled.
  • Google Firebase Analytics & Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymized usage events (such as screen views and feature interactions) and technical crash logs/stack traces to diagnose stability issues. No personal financial values entered into loan simulations are transmitted.
  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat (In-App Purchases): Manages the optional "Pro" unlock and purchase restoration. All payment processing is handled exclusively and securely by Google Play; JarmSoft never receives or stores your credit card or banking credentials.

7. GDPR Compliance (European Economic Area & UK)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the GDPR regarding data protection. Because Loan & Mortgage Calculator does not collect, process, or store personal data on remote servers:

  • We hold no personal data to access, rectify, or delete.
  • You retain 100% data portability and control, as all data is stored exclusively on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app deletes all local configurations immediately.

8. CCPA Compliance (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • We do not sell or share personal information of any consumer to third parties.
  • We collect zero categories of personal information.

9. Security of Your Data

Because all data remains exclusively on your device, the security of your loan calculations relies on your device's native operating system protections (biometrics, screen lock, and device encryption). We recommend keeping your operating system updated to ensure optimal device security.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain compliance with evolving Google Play policies and legal standards. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
